# Vendor Risk Questionnaire: Section & Domain Mapping

This document outlines the 20 assessment domains (A through T) containing the 61 evaluation criteria in the assessment tool.

---

## Domain Overview

* **Section A: AI Governance & Accountability** (Org structure, AI ethics policies, roles)
* **Section B: System Architecture & Model Documentation** (Model cards, architecture diagrams, training lineage)
* **Section C: Data Governance & Quality** (Data provenance, labeling standards, noise handling)
* **Section D: Data Privacy & Protection** (PII handling, anonymization, consent mechanisms)
* **Section E: Information Security Controls** (Encryption in transit/at rest, access controls, SOC 2 Type II)
* **Section F: Robustness & Reliability** (Stress testing, fallback mechanisms, edge-case failure modes)
* **Section G: Bias, Fairness & Non-Discrimination** (Disparate impact analysis, demographic parity checks)
* **Section H: Explainability & Transparency** (Interpretability tools, user disclosure mechanisms)
* **Section I: Human Oversight & Intervention** (Human-in-the-loop design, override capabilities)
* **Section J: Model Drift & Continuous Performance** (Performance degradation monitoring, retraining triggers)
* **Section K: AI Agent Permissions & Autonomy** (Permission scoping, tool-calling boundaries, egress restrictions)
* **Section L: Prompt Injection & Adversarial Risks** (Direct/indirect prompt injection defenses, jailbreak resilience)
* **Section M: Auditability, Logging & Telemetry** (Immutable audit logs, decision tracking, retention)
* **Section N: Incident Management & Vulnerability Disclosure** (SLA for security notifications, breach notification windows)
* **Section O: Model Change Management & Versioning** (Breaking change policies, model deprecation notices)
* **Section P: Subprocessors & Supply Chain Risk** (Fourth-party disclosures, upstream foundation model dependencies)
* **Section Q: Data Retention & Deletion** (Tenant data isolation, deletion commitments upon termination)
* **Section R: Business Continuity & Operational Resilience** (SLA commitments, disaster recovery, model redundancy)
* **Section S: Regulatory & Legal Compliance** (Jurisdictional legal compliance: EU AI Act, US state laws, DPDP Act)
* **Section T: Assurance Evidence & Third-Party Audit** (ISO 42001 certification, external red-teaming reports)
